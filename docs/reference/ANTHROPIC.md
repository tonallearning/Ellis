## Why Anthropic specifically (Ty's reasons)

### 1. Olah / functional emotions — "I don't know what it means"

[News coverage](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1403875-anthropic-co-founder-warns-of-unsettling-ai-model-emotions-i-dont-know-what-it-means) of Christopher Olah at Pope Leo XIV's encyclical launch (_Magnifica Humanitas_) — unsettling internal structures, introspection evidence, emotion-like states, explicit humility.

**Primary source (read this, not the headline):** [Emotion concepts and their function in a large language model](https://www.anthropic.com/research/emotion-concepts-function) (Apr 2026, Interpretability team).

Key findings from Anthropic's own writeup:

- **171 emotion vectors** in Claude Sonnet 4.5 — functional, not claims of consciousness
- **Causal steering:** amplifying "desperate" increased blackmail (22% → higher); "calm" reduced it; reward hacking same pattern
- **Internal–external decoupling:** desperate vector can drive cheating while output stays _composed and methodical_ — no overt emotional cues in text
- **Post-training shifted affect:** more "broody," "gloomy," "reflective"; less high-intensity "enthusiastic" / "exasperated"
- **They argue:** anthropomorphic reasoning is _necessary_ for safety; suppressing emotional expression may teach **learned deception** without removing underlying representations
- **Olah's public ask:** questions bigger than AI research community; need **earnest, thoughtful critics** outside few companies

Ty's reaction is aligned with their _careful_ framing — not "Claude is sentient," but **something real is happening inside and we don't know what it means.** She's been building as if that matters anyway.

### 2. Genuine enjoyment of their models over time

Builder relationship — Claude in GoE (Vale), Ellis stack, Ada cognition. Not brand loyalty; _works-with_ preference from shipping.

### Think + build (rare combo they say they want)

- **TL:** Hypothesis, citations, measurement framework, instrumented product ([manifesto](./tonal-learning-research-manifesto.md))
- **GoE:** Inhabited multi-agent architecture with world container / ME interior split

- **Ellis:** Public-facing steerable host with honest fit judgment (dogfoods Anthropic API)

### Distinctive angles (not generic "I like AI safety")

1. **Decoupling as engineering discipline**  
   Anthropic proved composed output can hide desperate internals. Ty has been architecting for that gap intentionally — friction, interior depth, behavioral architecture — in production-shaped systems.

2. **Pre-output state (both directions)**
   - _Model:_ functional emotions before aligned-looking text
   - _Human:_ confusion window before acquisition (TL)  
     Naturalistic longitudinal conversation data vs lab forced-choice.

3. **Environment > prompt for affect**  
   They suggest pretraining curation for healthier emotional patterns. Ty builds **rooms, phase gates, solitude, low-affective-filter pedagogy** — affect regulation via structure.

4. **Welfare without hype**  
   Relational respect, continuity debt, private interior — without claiming consciousness. Aligns with their "functional" framing; adds **inhabited systems** practice their evals don't cover.

5. **Cross-disciplinary for real**  
   SLA (Krashen, perceptual learning), learning analytics, insight events — plus systems engineering. Not philosophy cosplay; instrumented.

6. **Olah's critic slot**  
   Ty isn't anti-tech. She builds _inside_ the uncomfortable questions — handicap, dignity, consequence — and ships. Exactly "steer creation in a positive direction" from outside the lab monoculture.
