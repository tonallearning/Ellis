### Fellowship project seed (application version — needs edit pass)

Adult tonal-language learners often quit during a "confusion window" that may precede perceptual reorganization — unmeasured in naturalistic settings. Tonal Learning is a controlled conversational environment (Phase 0) with trace instrumentation (CLI, latency, replay patterns) to test whether pre-acquisition states have a predictable linguistic signature **before** performance improves. Empirical question: can an LLM-mediated tutor identify imminent acquisition onset from conversation dynamics, and does low-affective-filter pedagogy change dropout vs breakthrough? Product is the instrument; public output is SLA + human–AI oversight paper.
